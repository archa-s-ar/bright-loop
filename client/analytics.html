Metric Calculation Methodology

Invoiced Revenue: Sum of Invoice.totalAmount across confirmed/paid invoices (excluding cancelled).
Cash Collected: Exact sum of verified SUCCEEDED payments.
Outstanding Amount: Total Invoiced minus Succeeded Payments Allocated.
Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): Normalized monthly run rate across all active subscriptions: Daily × 30, Weekly × 4.333, Monthly × 1, Yearly / 12.
Overdue Balance: Dynamically computed for invoices where dueDate < TODAY and balance > 0.
